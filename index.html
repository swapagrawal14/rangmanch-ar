Director's Notes

Act I

You become the lead. The character is cut live from its own artwork and re-posed on your skeleton — shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips — every frame. Wave, dance, crouch, lean: it moves because you move. No pose pictures, no stickers.

Act II

Your face is its face. Open your mouth for its A, round your lips for O, smile for E — and when you blink, it blinks. No typing, no timeline. Just acting.

Act III

Hands conduct the co-actors. Raise a hand and a full-size co-actor walks onto the stage floor beside you. Move your hand to walk them around; pinch thumb to finger and they talk.

Finale

Press Record — the show begins. The film comes from the canvas, the voice from your mic, recorded together. When you stop, the film is ready to download and share.

🔒  The whole show runs on-device — the Pose, Hand and Face models live inside your phone. No video ever goes to a server. Ever.
