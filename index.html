Kaise act karein

  • Ek haath upar — main character wave karega · dono haath upar — open pose
  • Haath side mein failao — point pose · normal khade raho — base pose
  • Munh kholo / O banao / smile karo — character ka munh tumhare munh ke saath chalega (A / O / E), palak jhapkao toh woh bhi jhapkayega
  • Haathon ko camera ke paas lao — har haath ek chhota character ban jayega; angootha-ungli milao (pinch) toh woh bolega
  • Record Scene — tumhari awaaz mic se, film canvas se, ek saath record hogi
