Director's Notes

Act I

You are replaced, never shown. No camera image ever reaches the stage. The character stands where you stand — its face on your face at selfie distance, its whole body on yours when you step back — drawn from its own artwork, never cut up.

Act II

Your face is its face. Open your mouth for its A, round your lips for O, smile or laugh for a big E grin — and when you blink, it blinks.

Act III

Your hands pick its pose. Wave, point, throw both arms open or give a thumbs-up; put a hand over your mouth, a finger to your lips, a palm to your forehead or both hands on your head; press your palms together for a namaste — and the character performs its own drawing of that gesture, with whichever hand you use, even with the other one holding the phone. Tilt, turn, nod or shake your head and its head follows.

Finale

Press Record. The film comes from the stage, the voice from your mic. Save it as MP4 (or WebM on older browsers) or share it straight to WhatsApp or Instagram. Tick Co-actors for company: raise a hand and a co-actor walks on; pinch to make them talk.

🔒  Everything runs on your phone. The tracking models download once and run on-device; no video ever goes to a server.
